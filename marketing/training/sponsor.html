Sponsor guide

Your login is read-only and scoped automatically to your campaign. You cannot change anything, and you never see another brand's data, so there is nothing to set up and nothing to break. Use it to check today's numbers, watch promoters on shift, and pull your own reports without emailing the agency.

What you need: a browser, your portal username and password, and the address of the portal (your agency will give you this). Everything you see is live, refresh the page for the latest.
01

Sign in and read your campaign name

Every time you open the portal

  1. Open the portal address in a browser, enter your username and password, and sign in. You land on your Overview (the dashboard).

  2. Your login is locked to the one campaign you were granted — there is no switcher to operate. The top-right shows its name so you always know which campaign you are looking at.

  3. Pin the pages you use most. Hover a menu item and click its star (or click the star next to the page title at the top) and it appears under Favorites at the top of your menu. Open Personalization from your account menu (top-right) to reorder or remove favorites.

    Follows you: favorites are saved to your account, so you see the same menu on any computer or browser you sign in from.
02

Read the dashboard

Your at-a-glance view of today

  1. At the top is Day x of y campaign progress. The four tiles are today so far: Sales today, Active outlets (x of y), Conversion and Customers approached, each with a green or red change against yesterday. Last sales day shows the most recent earlier day that had sales, which may be several days ago. A brand-new campaign shows a Set up your campaign checklist until the first sales arrive.

  2. The Sales trend chart shows daily sales for the last 7 or 30 days; choose View as table for the figures. Top-performing outlets, Outlets with room to grow and Customer interest (products shoppers asked about but did not buy) cover the last 7 days. Sponsors do not see staff alerts or the live promoter map here; use Today → Live Locations in the sidebar for that.

  3. Top 10 products by sales lists your best sellers for the campaign so far, with units and value. For anything more detailed, use the reports (task 6).

03

Watch the live promoter map

To see who is on shift right now

  1. Open Today → Live Locations from the left menu for the full-screen version, with a list of everyone checked in and when. Click a row in the list to highlight that promoter's marker on the map — click again to clear the highlight.

  2. A promoter only appears while their app is open and they are checked in. An empty map after hours is normal.

04

Check who is on shift

Promoter and supervisor attendance

  1. Open Today → Attendance for the promoter check-in / check-out log: outlet, date, check-in and check-out time, and on-time / late status. Filter by outlet or date range, and export to Excel.

  2. Open Today → Supervisor Attendance for the same thing for supervisors — their own check-in and check-out at the outlets they visit.

05

See where people have been

Historical GPS trails, not the live map

  1. Open Reports → Promoter Tracking (or Reports → Supervisor Tracking) to review one person's GPS breadcrumb trail on a chosen date, drawn as a coloured path on a map above a time table. Clicking a table row highlights that exact breadcrumb on the map above. This is history, not the live view from task 3.

06

Pull a sales report

By product or by brand, any dates, exportable

  1. From the left menu, open Reports → Client Reports and choose Overall Outlet and SKU Wise (per product) or Brand Wise (per outlet and brand).

  2. The report opens on the whole campaign to date. To look at a shorter period, set a From and To date — it updates straight away, or press Refresh.

  3. Each row shows units sold and total value; the grand total is at the bottom. The Brand Wise report rolls the same numbers up per outlet and brand.

  4. Press the green Excel button to download the table.

07

Dig into the sales log

Row-by-row, per outlet and day

  1. Open Reports → Client Reports → Overall Outlet and SKU Wise. This is the raw log, one row per product, per outlet, per day, with unit price, start quantity, sold, and a closing Total Sales column (sold quantity × unit price).

  2. Open Reports → Starting Stock to see the stock each promoter started the day with — date, outlet, promoter, product, unit price and Start Qty — for your campaign, and the same page is your downloadable starting-stock report. It is the opening stock from their first stock update after checking in, so a mid-day restock does not change it. Only days from the release onward are listed. Filter by outlet and date, and download with Excel.

  3. Filter by Outlet and a date range (it updates straight away, or press Refresh), or type in Search. Export with Excel.
