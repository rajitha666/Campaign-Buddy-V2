Admin guide

You run everything from here: the client catalog, the campaign, the outlets, the staff, and every posting. Once a campaign is set up your day is mostly monitoring, who checked in, what is selling, what needs correcting. Tasks 2 to 9 are the one-time setup; tasks 10 to 15 are the day-to-day.

Before you start: know your client, the products in scope, the outlets (with rough GPS), and your promoter and supervisor roster. Do the setup tasks in order, each one builds on the last.
01

Sign in and the campaign switcher

Every session

  1. Sign in with your username and password. You land on the Dashboard.

  2. The campaign switcher in the top-right (the folder) controls almost every screen, activations, attendance, sales, tracking, reports all follow it. The catalog, outlets, cities, staff pool and users are not campaign-bound and stay reachable whatever is selected.

    Habit: glance at the switcher before you read any numbers or make any edit.
02

Set up the catalog

One-time, client, brand, then products

The catalog is shared across all campaigns for that client, set a product up once and reuse it.

  1. Open Clients and add the brand owner: company name, contact, phone, email, address.

  2. Open Products → Brands and add a brand for that client.

  3. Open Products → List and add each SKU: name, brand, SKU code, unit price in whole rupees, re-order level, and an image if you have one.

03

Cities and outlets

One-time, add new locations before the campaign

  1. Open Outlets → Cities and add any city you need (name, province, district). Outlets and staff both reference this list.

  2. Open Outlets → List and add each retail location: name, contact person, address, city, phone, and its GPS coordinates (use the geocode helper). The coordinates and the geofence radius (150 m by default) are what verify a promoter's check-in.

04

Create the campaign

One per activation programme

  1. Open Campaigns → List and add the campaign: number, name, client, description, and a start and end date. Creating it grants you full access to it automatically.

  2. The status follows the dates on its own (upcoming → active → ended). You can override it, for example to end a campaign early, and the override sticks until you change the dates.

  3. Open the campaign's Campaign Items and pick the SKUs that are sellable anywhere in this campaign. Search by name or SKU, tick as many as you need, and Add Selected links them all in one batch (or create-and-link a brand new product in the same step).

05

Add your staff

Promoters and supervisors

  1. Open Staff → List and add each person: employee ID, full name, Display Name (what they see in the app), type (promoter or supervisor), mobile number, home city, "reports to" for leave approval, and their emergency and bank details.

  2. Under Login, set a password. The promoter signs in on the app with either their mobile number (any common format) or an app username. For a supervisor who also needs the portal, link a portal login here.

06

Create activations

One per promoter, per outlet, the heart of the setup

  1. With the right campaign selected, open Campaigns → Activation and add one. An activation is one promoter, at one outlet, for this campaign, over one date range.

  2. Set the outlet, promoter, an optional named supervisor, the distributor point if any, the date range, the shift window (for on-time / late), and the three target settings (item- or brand-wise, daily or monthly, by units or by value). Outlet, promoter, supervisor and distributor point all show the person's name (and employee ID, for staff) and are searchable, so a long roster stays easy to pick from. The date range starts pre-filled with the campaign's own dates — adjust it if this activation runs shorter.

    The auto-grant: naming a supervisor here gives their portal login access to that outlet on this campaign, automatically. It only ever widens their access, and the portal confirms it.

  3. Open the activation's Activation Items and tick which of the campaign's products this outlet actually stocks (there is an "add all" shortcut).

  4. Open Activation Targets and set a numeric target per item over a date range, with an optional repeat.

07

Custom sales fields

Optional, extra data points promoters record each day

  1. Open Sales → Custom Fields and add a field for this campaign: a label, an applies-to scope (Whole day, one value per promoter per day, like weather; or Each product, one value per SKU per day, like damaged units), a type (number / text / yes-no / dropdown), a Required flag, and a sort order.

  2. A Required field blocks the promoter's Confirm & submit until it is filled.

    Note: once a field has recorded values, its type and scope lock. To retire it, archive it: the history is kept, and it is just hidden from the entry forms.
08

Give supervisors, sponsors and admins access

Per campaign, for the people who watch it or help run it

  1. Open the campaign's Campaign Access tab. Add a portal user, choose their outlet scope (all outlets or a named subset), and save. This is how a sponsor gets their read-only view.

  2. Supervisors usually get their access from the activation auto-grant (task 6), but you can also grant or widen it here. To revoke, remove the grant.

    Reminder: supervisor and sponsor logins are read-only on the server; they cannot change anything regardless of what any screen shows.

  3. A new Campaign Admin joining partway through a campaign doesn't need Super Admin to step in: from Campaigns → List, open the campaign's Admins action (the people icon). Search for their existing account by name, username or email and Link Admin — or, if they don't have one yet, fill in + New Admin Account to create and link it in the same step.

    Who can do this: Super Admin, or any Campaign Admin already linked to that campaign. A campaign can have any number of admins — linking a new one never removes another, and the same account can be linked to several campaigns at once.
09

Supervisor routes and QA

Optional, plan visits and the visit checklist

  1. Open Supervisors → Assign Routes. Pick a supervisor, a set of outlets (only outlets already active on this campaign), and a date range. The month calendar shows the plan. This is planning only, it does not drive check-in.

  2. Open Supervisors → Tasks to build the per-campaign QA checklist template: a category, a task type (range or feedback), and the question text, across the nine QA categories.

10

Your daily view

Every morning

  1. Start on the Dashboard: active outlets today, sales today, month-to-date, top items, and the live map. Check Products → Reorder for anything the promoters have flagged as low.

  2. Open Staff → Attendance for the check-in log with on-time / late badges. A blank check-in time means the promoter has not started.

  3. Open Staff → Absence and pick today. It lists everyone with an activation today but no check-in, flagged "on leave" or "no check-in".

  4. Open Sales → Sales Update Status and pick a date to see which activations have submitted their sales ("Completed") and which have not ("Missing"), with footfall.

11

Correct a promoter's sales

Without a site visit

  1. Open Sales → Update Sales. Work down the cascading dropdowns: Outlet, then Promoter, then Activation, then Date. Press Load sales.

  2. That day's grid loads. Edit opening stock or sold quantity for any row, and any day-level or per-product custom fields, then save. Raising opening stock mid-day is allowed (a restock).

  3. The correction is attributed to head office; the promoter's confirmed summary stays locked to them.

    Why it just works: every total is computed from the raw counters on read, so the dashboard, reports and the promoter's performance all show the corrected figure the next time they load. There is no cached number to chase.
12

Approve leave

As requests come in

  1. Open Staff → Leave Requests. Each row shows the employee, dates, reason and status. Approve or decline inline, "approved by" defaults to the requester's "reports to" person. The promoter sees the decision in the app.

13

Tracking

Where your people are

  1. Tracking → Seller Live Locations, a live map of everyone checked in, refreshing on its own, with a list of who and since when.

  2. Tracking → Promoter (or Supervisor), the GPS breadcrumb trail for one person on one date, as a coordinate-and-time table.

  3. Supervisors → Outlet Attendance, the supervisor visit log, cross-checked against the promoters' attendance for the same outlets and dates.

14

Reports and performance

During the campaign and at the end

  1. Sales → SKU Wise Sales and Sales → Outlet Wise, the raw log and the per-outlet rollup, both filterable and exportable.

  2. Reports → Overall SKU Wise / Overall Brand Wise, campaign totals by product or brand, with grand totals and Excel export.

  3. Admin Reports → Monthly Attendance, a per-promoter day grid for a chosen month (present / absent / leave / not-started), exportable to CSV.

  4. Staff → Profiles, a performance evaluation for one promoter or supervisor. Search the Staff Member picker by name — it shows each person's type alongside their name so a long roster stays easy to scan. You get their full HR profile (contact, city, reports-to, emergency and bank details), which activation(s) they're on today (or their most recent one), performance and attendance %, most-sold products, a sales-and-customers-approached chart across their activation history, and a table of every past activation.

15

Users, roles and licence usage

Super Admin

  1. Users, create portal logins (username, password, display name, email, role). Campaign access is not set here; it is granted per campaign (task 8) or by the supervisor auto-grant.

  2. Roles, view the four roles (Super Admin, Campaign Admin, Supervisor, Sponsor) and what each can do. Read-only enforcement is on the server; this screen is for reference.

  3. License Usage, seat usage per campaign across the four groups (promoters, supervisors, admins, sponsors) against each cap, with weekly and monthly history and an alert as a campaign nears its limit. Limits are advisory: going over never blocks an assignment. Edit caps is Super Admin only.
