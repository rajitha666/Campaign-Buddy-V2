Capability brief

Run the whole in-store activation on one platform.

Functional specification, operational scenarios, and the commercial model.

Campaign Buddy is a field-marketing execution platform for agencies that run in-store product activations. One backend serves a field app that every promoter and supervisor works from, and a web portal where head office runs operations and sponsors watch their campaign live. This brief is written for the people evaluating it: what it does, how it behaves, and how it is priced.

Campaign BuddyCapability brief
At a glance

What it replaces, and what it adds.

Campaign Buddy replaces the mix of WhatsApp photos, paper stock sheets and end-of-week spreadsheets that most activations still run on. In their place: verified check-ins, stock and sales updated through the day, a live map of promoters on shift, and a portal the agency's own clients can log into.

The two products

ProductWho works in itWhat it does
CB OfficeHead office, supervisors, sponsorsWeb portal. Set up campaigns, staff and outlets; watch attendance, location and sales live; correct data; pull reports.
CB MobilePromoters & field supervisorsField app on the phone staff already carry. Check in and out, update stock and sales per SKU, log footfall, confirm the day.

The four people who log in

  • Head office / campaign admin. Configures everything, corrects data, runs reports.
  • Field supervisor. Read-only portal view of their outlets, plus CB Mobile for their own outlet visits.
  • Sponsor / brand. Read-only portal, auto-scoped to their campaign: dashboards, live map, self-serve reports.
  • Promoter. CB Mobile only; the daily shift workflow.

What you have on day one

  • GPS check-in and check-out, with the check-in verified against each outlet's geofence
  • Stock and units sold per SKU, updated live, with lost-demand and re-order signals
  • A live map of every checked-in promoter, refreshing every ~15 seconds
  • Self-serve reporting for sponsors, scoped automatically to their campaign
  • Staff management: attendance, leave, supervisor routes and QA, per-promoter performance
  • Data corrections from the portal, without a site visit
  • SKU-, brand- and outlet-level reports, exportable to Excel
  • Per-campaign seat metering, with weekly and monthly usage history in the portal

Figures and names used throughout this brief ("Radiance Q3 Push", "Nawala Retail Outlet", promoter names) are an illustrative sample campaign, not real data.

Access model

Who sees what, and why the numbers hold.

RoleWrite accessScope of visibility
Super AdminadmEverythingEvery campaign in the system, bypassing access grants entirely.
Campaign AdminusrEverythingOnly the campaigns they hold a grant for.
SupervisorsupervisorRead-onlyTheir granted campaign(s), often narrowed to a named subset of outlets.
SponsorsponsorRead-onlyTheir granted campaign(s), usually all outlets on it.

Access is granted per campaign, for all outlets or a named subset. Naming a field supervisor on an activation automatically grants their portal login access to that outlet on that campaign. It is always additive, always outlet-scoped, and the portal confirms it. Access only ever widens, never narrows.

Load-bearing behaviour

  1. Geofence is a soft flag. A check-in is never blocked by location. Outside the outlet's radius (150 m default), the record is marked "location unverified" for review.
  2. Ten-minute grace. A check-in is on-time within 10 minutes of the planned shift start, late after.
  3. One open shift at a time, anywhere. A promoter can be assigned to concurrent activations but checked in to only one, which prevents double-claimed shifts.
  4. Totals are computed on read, never stored. Sales, roll-ups and remaining stock are calculated from the raw daily counters, so there are no stale cached numbers to reconcile.
  5. Mid-day restock edits opening stock in place. There is never a second stock row for the same item and day.
  6. A confirmed daily summary is locked to the promoter. After "Confirm & submit" only head office can amend that day, via Update Sales.
  7. Custom fields freeze once they hold values. Type and scope lock; a retired field is archived with its history kept, never deleted.
  8. Read-only roles are rejected on every write endpoint, server-side, regardless of what any screen shows.

Campaign BuddyThe practicalities
The practicalities

How it's built, run, and priced.

Technology

Backend: Node.js, TypeScript, Express, PostgreSQL, Prisma. Two independent JWT token spaces (field staff and portal users). Runtime validation on every write route; errors returned as plain, field-named sentences. An automated test suite covers the load-bearing rules: backend integration plus portal and mobile unit tests. Portal: React + Vite. Mobile: React Native / Expo SDK 57 (RN 0.86, React 19.2), TanStack Query.

Deployment

Ships with Docker Compose and a single canonical installation guide. Runs on your infrastructure or hosted for you, so your data stays where you choose. Whole-rupee pricing and Asia/Colombo time are the defaults.

Commercial model

  • Priced per campaign, against seat allowances for the four user groups: promoters, supervisors, admins and sponsors.
  • Metering is built in. CB Office tracks seat usage per campaign against each allowance, with weekly and monthly history and an in-portal alert as a campaign nears its limit. A seat counts from the moment an account is assigned; usage is captured as the peak for the period.
  • Allowances are soft. Going over never blocks an assignment; it changes the reported state, so a campaign is never held up by a licence question mid-run.
  • Pilot then rollout. Start on one live campaign at pilot terms; move the rest of the book across once it's proven.
  • Included: both apps, all four roles, updates, onboarding for your ops team, and support.
  • Figures are quoted per engagement once campaign volume and team size are known.
