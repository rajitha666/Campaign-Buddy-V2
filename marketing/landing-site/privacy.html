Campaign Buddy is a field-marketing platform used by agencies that run in-store product activations. It has two parts: CB Office (a web portal for agency managers and their clients) and CB Mobile (an app for the promoters and supervisors who work in stores). This policy explains what personal data the product handles, why, who can see it, and what choices you have.

Who is responsible for your data

Each agency uses its own separate Campaign Buddy deployment. Your agency (your employer or the company that engaged you) decides what data is entered about you and why, and is the controller of that data. [Dyro Technologies, registered address] builds and operates the software and handles the data on the agency's behalf. Accounts are created by your agency. There is no public sign-up.

Location

The mobile app asks permission to use your phone's location. It collects your position when you check in and while you are checked in for a shift, so your campaign managers can confirm attendance at the right outlet. It does not collect location after you check out. You can refuse permission in your phone settings, but check-in needs a location fix, so you will not be able to start a shift without it. Location is shown only to the managers your agency has given access to that campaign.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use your data to make automated decisions about you.

Who can see your data

Access is by role. Your agency's administrators can see staff records for their campaigns. Supervisors see their own outlets. A client's sponsor users see campaign results (sales, attendance and reports) for their own campaigns. National ID numbers, dates of birth, addresses and bank details are shown only to your agency's administrators, not to supervisors or clients. Every sign-in to the portal needs a username and password, and passwords are stored in hashed form, never in plain text. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Your rights

Under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, you have the right to know what data is held about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and to ask for data to be erased where it is no longer needed. Please make requests to your agency first. You can also write to us at [privacy contact email] and we will pass requests to the right agency or deal with them ourselves if they concern the software itself.
