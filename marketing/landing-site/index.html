Staff management

Keep the field honest, and keep promoters informed.

Simple rules that stop shifts being gamed, and workflows that keep the team running without a phone call.

One open shift at a time

A promoter can be assigned to several activations, but checked in to only one at a time. No double claimed shifts.

On time versus late

Every check-in is compared with the shift start, allowing a short grace period, and marked on time or late.

Leave, requested from the shift

Promoters ask for time off in the app, their manager approves it in the portal, and the answer shows up on the phone.

Supervisor routes and QA

Plan a supervisor's outlet visits on a calendar, and hold them to a quality checklist for each campaign.

Performance per promoter

Sales, attendance and best day for each promoter, over any dates.

Location checks flag, never block

A check-in away from the outlet is never blocked. It is marked "location unverified" for a person to review.
