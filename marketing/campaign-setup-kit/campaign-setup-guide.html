Campaign Setup Guide

This guide walks through everything we need to set up your first campaign — what each field means, why we ask for it, and what a good answer looks like. The actual data goes into the companion spreadsheet, not this document.

📊 Fill in "Campaign Setup Workbook.xlsx" — it has one tab per section below, in the same order, ready for you to type straight into. Use this guide alongside it if a field isn't obvious. When you're done, send the workbook back to your Campaign Buddy contact.
1 · About your organisationWorkbook tab: "1. Client"

Company details

One record per client. If Campaign Buddy already manages another campaign for you, just give us the company name — we'll attach this new campaign to your existing record instead of creating a duplicate.

Company name *
The legal or trading name we'll show on your campaign records.e.g. Radiance Cosmetics (Pvt) Ltd
Primary contact person *
Who we go to with questions about this campaign.
Contact numberoptional
A phone number for that contact.
Emailoptional
Used for setup correspondence — not a login.
Business addressoptional
Registered or head-office address.

3 · ProductsWorkbook tab: "3. Products"

Products to track

Every item promoters will sell or stock-check, grouped under the brands from Section 2.

SKU / codeoptional
Your own product code. Leave blank and we'll generate one.
Product name *
What promoters and reports will show.
Unit price (LKR)optional
Used to calculate sales value against targets.
Reorder leveloptional
Stock count that should trigger a low-stock flag. Defaults to 0 (off) if left blank.
Supplier / descriptionoptional
Free text — photos can be added later in the portal.

4 · CampaignWorkbook tab: "4. Campaign"

Campaign details

One workbook covers one campaign. If you're setting up more than one at the same time, use a separate copy of the workbook for each.

Campaign name *
Shows across the portal and mobile app.e.g. Radiance Q3 Push
Descriptionoptional
A short line on what the campaign is for.
Start / end date *
Sets the campaign's active window; status (upcoming / active / ended) updates automatically from these dates.
Timezoneoptional
Defaults to Asia/Colombo — only change if the campaign runs elsewhere.
Seat limitsoptional
How many promoter / supervisor / admin / sponsor logins the campaign allows. Defaults are 20 / 5 / 2 / 2 — only fill in if you need higher limits; going over a limit never blocks you, it just raises a warning.
5 · OutletsWorkbook tab: "5. Outlets"

Outlets / stores

Every location a promoter or supervisor will be stationed at.

Outlet name / address / city *
Enough for us to identify and geotag the location.
Contact person, phone, mobileoptional
In-store contact for the promoter or supervisor.
Geofence radius (m)optional
How far from the pinned location a check-in is still accepted. Defaults to 150m. This is a soft flag only — check-in is never blocked, just marked unverified if outside range.

The exact map pin is dropped later by your Campaign Admin from the portal — an address and city is enough to get started.

6 · Distributor pointsWorkbook tab: "6. Distributor Points"

Distributor / warehouse points

Optional — only needed if activations restock from a distributor rather than the outlet's own store room. Skip this tab if not applicable.

7 · Field staffWorkbook tab: "7. Field Staff"

Promoters & supervisors

Anyone who will use the CB Mobile app on the ground.

Full name, role *
Role is either Promoter or Supervisor.
Mobile number, based in (city)optional
Helps us route routes and shifts sensibly.
Reports tooptional
For promoters, the supervisor who oversees them.

Employee ID and mobile login password are generated automatically when the account is created. Extra HR details — NIC, date of birth, address, emergency contact, bank account — are not needed now; they can be filled in later, person by person, from each Staff Profile in the portal.

8 · ActivationsWorkbook tab: "8. Activations"

Activations

An activation is one promoter assigned to one outlet for a date range — it's what actually appears on the mobile app and the field calendar.

Outlet, promoter, start / end date *
The minimum needed to create the assignment.
Supervisor, distributor point, shift timesoptional
Fill in where they apply.
Target type / periodoptional
Whether targets are tracked per item or per brand overall, and whether they reset daily or monthly. Leave blank to use the campaign default (per item, daily) — only fill in for an activation that should work differently.
9 · TargetsWorkbook tab: "9. Targets"

Sales targets

Optional at setup — targets can be added or adjusted from the portal at any time once activations are running, so it's fine to leave this tab blank and set targets later.

Target value, measured in
Either a unit count ("units sold") or a sales value in LKR.
Repeats each period?
"Yes" re-applies the same target every day/month for the length of the activation; "No" applies it once, over the date range given.
10 · Daily reportsWorkbook tab: "10. Custom Fields"

Extra fields for daily reports

Beyond the standard sales and stock numbers, tell us if promoters should record anything else each day — e.g. "competitor price seen", "display compliant Y/N", "customer feedback". Leave the tab blank if the standard report is enough; this can also be set up later.

Type
Number, Text, Yes/No, or Dropdown (with your own list of options).
Captured per
"Day overall" asks once per activation per day; "Per product" asks once for every product being tracked, each day.
11 · Office loginsWorkbook tab: "11. Portal Logins"

Admin & sponsor logins

People who need to log into CB Office (the web portal) rather than the mobile app — your Campaign Admin, and any client-side sponsors who should see reporting. Field staff from Section 7 don't need an entry here; they log in on the mobile app instead.

Role
Campaign Admin can edit everything in the campaign. Sponsor is read-only reporting access.
Outlet access
"All outlets" sees the whole campaign; "Specific outlets" restricts a sponsor to just the outlets you list — handy for a client who should only see their own region.
12 · Anything elseWorkbook tab: "12. Notes"

Notes for your Campaign Buddy contact

Special requirements, go-live date pressure, existing data to migrate, or anything not covered above — free text, in the workbook.

What happens after you send the workbook back

  1. Dyro Technologies creates your client, brand and product records, and your outlets, from tabs 1–6.
  2. We create the campaign and its first activations (tabs 4, 8–10), and the staff and portal logins (tabs 7 & 11).
  3. Your Campaign Admin receives their CB Office login and can immediately edit, add to, or correct anything above — outlets, staff, targets, custom fields and sponsor access all stay fully editable after go-live.
