Five stages, end to end, and Campaign Buddy carries all of them.

From the first client record to the final brand report, the whole activation programme lives in one place.

Build the campaign

Staff management

Keep the field honest, and keep promoters informed.

The rules that stop shifts being gamed, and the workflows that keep a team of promoters and supervisors running without a phone call.

One open shift at a time

A promoter can be assigned to concurrent activations, but checked in to only one, anywhere. No double claimed shifts.

On time versus late

Every check-in is timed against the planned shift start plus a 10 minute grace, and recorded as on time or late.

Leave, requested from the shift

Promoters submit time off from the app, the "reports to" person approves it in the portal, and the decision shows back up on the phone.

Supervisor routes and QA

Plan a supervisor's outlet visits on a calendar, and hold them to a per campaign QA checklist across nine categories.

Performance per promoter

Overall performance, attendance, total sales, best day and brand contribution over any date range.

Geofence is a soft flag

A check-in outside the radius is never blocked. It is marked "location unverified" for a human to review.

The engineering underneath

Modern where it helps. Boring where it counts.

A field platform is only as good as the numbers it produces. These are the choices that keep them trustworthy.

Roles enforced on the serverA supervisor or sponsor token is rejected on every write endpoint, regardless of what any screen shows.
Totals computed, never storedSales, roll ups and remaining stock are calculated on read from the raw daily counters, so there are no stale cached numbers.
Every write is validatedRuntime validation on every write route, with errors that come back as plain sentences naming the field, safe to show a user directly.
Sessions that do not dropCB Mobile refreshes its token in the background, so a promoter is never signed out mid shift.
Automated test suiteIntegration tests across the backend plus unit tests on the portal and app cover the load bearing business rules.
Additive access onlyThe supervisor auto grant widens access and confirms it in the interface. It never silently removes a grant.

The parts that face the field feel new. The parts that hold the data are deliberately unexciting.
